**Refuge**

A place of refuge and companionship is a rare experience for one taking the trials. The inability to understand common speech or speak it are a grueling part of trials that can quickly drive a Primordial traveler to loneliness or despair. The guild has worked with the travelers over many centuries to unravel the ancient language and has set up taverns and inns with workers who have learnt to speak primordial. These places are still few and far apart but can provide a Primordial traveler with a brief escape from their lonely journey.

**The roads**

Those who welcome the help of the Road Keepers are not quite escorted, but may call upon their aid should they fall under attack of God Hunters. Travelers are given a small wand that can fire off a special fire bolt colored white and red. The guild’s spotters, located near trial locations and their routes, then relay their location to nearby members of the guild to allow for quick assistance. Spotter towers have come under attack at numerous occasions and to counteract this the guild has opted to train their spotters and scouts to climb tall trees and overlooks to ensure their service is uninterrupted. Spotting towers are still actively used, but the guild is not reliant exclusively on the towers to send help to travelers. Additionally, the decline in people taking the Primordial trials has led to the Road keepers simply serving their implied role. The roads that are most traveled by the Primordial travelers are now kept save, regardless of who travels them. This has made them more attractive to merchants travelling from one city to another, as it near guarantees the safety of their goods. Sometimes merchants will even take a path under the watchful eye of the Road Keepers even if a shorter path exists.

**Certified energy sources**

A registered Primordial traveler may choose to offer their gem to their family or the guild for use as an energy source or otherwise. This is currently the only way to guarantee that others know your use of a primordial gem is of ethical and consensual nature. It is often recommended that the owners of these gems set up their shops in major towns and cities to be safer from those looking to get their hands on the gems. Any gold procured from the selling of these gems is used to improve the guild and pay its members. The Guild hands the users of sold or granted gems certificates for each gem they are given, though it is rare for a single business to own more than one such gem. Additionally, a symbol of the Road Keepers is placed on the doorsteps of these businesses as a show to others, both that their energy is certified and that they are under the protection of the guild. These symbols are often forged by businesses that have obtained their Primordial gems through lesser means. The guild has its agents patrol cities near its bases on a regular basis to root out these imposters, which are fined according to law. The symbols of the guild have grown in complexity over the years as forgeries have gotten better. Currently, the symbols involve secret messages uncovered by flame as well as several types of magic enchantments, among which is a tracking magic.
