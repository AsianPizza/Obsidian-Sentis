# [[Good and neutral gods]]

Good and neutral gods are born after completing the Trial of Primordials. A series of supernatural trials that test the dedication and willpower of its takers to the absolute limit. Only those who can turn their desperation during these trials into salvation are granted the highest honour of Sentis. Upon the death of a victor of the Road of Despair, their bodies vanish into nothingness and their spirit ascends to godhood. Those undertaking the trial are often called Primordial travellers, and when someone says another person “walks the road” it refers to the Road of Despair. 



## Starting the trials

The trials can be started by visiting any of the primordial temples or a place of great elemental influence where their presence is strong. Once there, a participant must announce their desire to become a god and what it is they wish to accomplish as a god. The Primordials may deny their participation if they deem a candidate unfit or if they find malice in their words and soul. During the trials, any who bear the markings, whether primordial or void, can speak and hear only primordial. Other languages sound like confusing, distant blabber.

## Primordial markings

When a participant is accepted, their trials start immediately as they are branded with primordial energy across their entire body. These ancient tattoos are not only a sign of their participation, they also mark their progress. As a trial is completed, the tattoos light up in their elemental colours and nature. The trials of nature may see their markings start glowing green and plant life growing more vibrant in their step, where the markings of air will glow white and lace themselves with lightning. Upon the premature death of a Primordial traveller who has not finished their trials, a strange phenomenon occurs. The markings and their energies start fusing with the heart and soul of the Primordial traveller as they disintegrate and leave only a powerful gem behind. These gems containing their souls have empowering effects on the elements they are used with. The value of these gems has increased over the years, as people have found increasingly effective uses for them in various practices such as smithing, water mills, Alchemy and more. They are sought after by organizations looking to use them for power, and a main motivation for the God Hunters guild.

  

# [[Evil gods]]

Can start trials by simply speaking their dark desires to ascend to godhood into the deepest darkness that they can find. The void will answer by marking their bodies with violent streaks of black tattoos wrapping wildly around their bodies and cursing them with primordial speech and hearing similar to their heroic counterparts. Void Walkers, as they are called by others, for their black tattoos and their dark eyes seeming to suck any light into an endless void. Their trials are much simpler in nature. In order to complete their trials and ascend to godhood, they must slay Primordial Travelers until they have stolen enough of their markings to complete their own. This has made the early stages of the Primordial trials more dangerous, as Void Walkers will choose an easier prey over one who has completed multiple trials, afraid of their potential strength. Choosing to kill a Traveller with more completed trials, however, will significantly shorten their path to becoming a god.

## God Hunters

There is a darker side to these markings, however, as some have started hunting the bearers of primordial markings for sport and for the resource their deaths bring. A faction of God Hunters makes a competition and a mocking of Walkers of the Road. Granting their members merit and titles based on the number of lit markings of their quarry. The Hunters openly show off their kill count in various ways, some tattoo the number of completed markings of their kills on the face or neck, while others engrave their clothes or weapons with similar numbers. The God Hunters are reviled by normal folk, unwelcome in all but the most repugnant establishments. However, the greater their kill count, the more they are feared by people.

  

## [[Road keepers]]

Protectors of the Road have also risen in response to the God Hunters’ foul deeds. The Road keepers are an order of capable people who vow to protect the Primordial Travelers from the threat of the God Hunters. A traveller can apply for their protection if they bear the markings of the trials to make their trials just a little bit safer. There have been large numbers of travellers, however, who consider the God Hunters part of their trial. They will refuse help of any kind on the Road of Despair, deeming themselves too weak to ascend to godhood should they die by the hands of the God Hunters.
